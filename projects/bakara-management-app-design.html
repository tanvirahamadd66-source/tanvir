Bakara Management — Farm & Family Finance App

UI/UX Design App Design Dashboard Design Full-Stack Development Bilingual UI

A complete product design and full-stack build for Bakara Management — a bilingual (Bangla/English) farm and family finance platform, designed and shipped as one connected system across a responsive website and a native Android app. The scope covered every layer: multi-farm budget tracking, category-based income & expense entry, monthly family accounting with historical browsing, automated financial email reports, and secure authentication (email/password plus Google Sign-In) — all wrapped in a warm, illustrated visual identity.
