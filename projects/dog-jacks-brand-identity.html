Dog Jacks — Brand Identity & Style Guide

Brand Identity Brand Style Guide Logo Design Pet Branding Visual Identity

DOG JACKS is a premium dog-care brand focused on providing complete solutions for dogs under one roof. The identity system includes a logo with an integrated paw icon, a color palette of deep green (#0F4C38) and orange (#E77817), and typography guidelines built around Acumin Variable Concept — extended across packaging, billboards, merchandise, outdoor flags, mobile interface, stickers and lanyards for a consistent brand experience.
