Brand Identity

PasselTerra — Brand Identity

Brand Identity Logo Design Visual Identity

PasselTerra is a land-for-sale platform built on trust, clarity, and professionalism. The identity centers on a price-tag mark rendered in layered land tones — a warm sunset gradient over deep greens — symbolizing land, growth, and value. The system spans a primary and simplified secondary mark, a dedicated dark-mode variant, and a full multi-color favicon/icon set, backed by a complete brand style guide covering usage, spacing, color and typography.
